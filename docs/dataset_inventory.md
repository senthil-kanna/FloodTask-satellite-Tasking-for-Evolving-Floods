# Dataset inventory and provenance

Data status categories: **OBS** = historical observation; **REANALYSIS** = retrospective model reconstruction
(not available in real time); **SYNTH** = synthetic, generated by this project; **DERIVED** = computed here from the
others. No archived numerical weather **forecast** is used anywhere (see gap G1).

## Integrity
- `data/raw/sen1floods11/manifest.json`: SHA-256 for all 2230 downloaded files; verify with
  `python -m floodtask.data.sen1floods11 --verify`.
- `results/data_manifest.json` (generated by `scripts/make_manifest.py`): checksums of DEM tiles, scenes, ERA5 and
  real-event files.

## Gaps and alternatives
- **G1 Archived forecasts.** ECMWF TIGGE needs registration (declined for this project). The Open-Meteo Previous Runs
  API returned no precipitation at 1-3 day lead for June 2022 over Assam (checked 2026-10-20; coverage mostly begins
  2024). Alternative: synthetic forecast error models (simulation) and ERA5 pseudo-forecasts (replay), clearly labelled,
  plus "forecast missing" evaluations.
- **G2 River gauges.** No openly downloadable gauge series for the scenes (India-WRIS/CWC data need registration;
  GRDC needs a request). Alternative: a simulated gauge in simulation; gauges are treated as missing in replay.
- **G3 Multi-date labels.** No multi-date hand labels exist. The replay reference = agreement of a U-Net ensemble (seeds 1+2)
  with a VH Otsu threshold. Disagreeing cells are excluded. It is checked against hand labels on 2016-08-12 only.
- **G4 Authoritative flood maps.** NRSC/ISRO Bhuvan and UNOSAT products for Assam exist as maps/PDFs and portal layers,
  not as openly downloadable rasters for these dates. They are not used. This is a known limitation of the real-event reference.
