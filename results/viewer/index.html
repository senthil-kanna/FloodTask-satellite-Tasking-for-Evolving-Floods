Where RL is used. At every pass the recurrent PPO agent picks 16 of the 256 tiles, one at a time, from per-tile inputs: current flood estimate and its uncertainty, hours since last imaged, observed and forecast rain, terrain and satellite access. The greedy panel uses the same map-building and budget with a hand-written rule.
How the maps are made. Cells inside imaged tiles take the segmented flood class (U-Net on Sentinel-1) fused with a terrain prior, plus a confidence. Cells never imaged stay unknown. Data age is hours since the tile was last imaged.
Outlook. 48 runs of the flood model with different physical settings, driven by rain observed so far and the rain forecast issued at the pass, weighted by how well each run matches the PPO map now. Shading is the share of runs that flood a cell. In the simulated regions the truth comes from the same flood model, so outlook scores there are a best case. The Assam replays are the real test (see Real events).
Truth. Assam: model-derived reference masks (U-Net ensemble AND VH-Otsu; cells where they disagree are excluded, hatched). Simulated regions: the hidden flood state of the simulator. The Assam forecast is an ERA5 pseudo-forecast built after the event.

How to read this

Definitions used on every tab.

Mean per-pass IoU: overlap between the agent's flood map and the reference, averaged over the 12 passes of an episode and only over passes where flooding is present. Primary endpoint, fixed before testing.
Final IoU: the same overlap after the last pass.
Paired difference: per scene, PPO minus comparator, averaged over episode seeds and the 5 training seeds.
Holm p: Wilcoxon signed-rank p over scenes, corrected for the 7 planned comparisons.
Oracle: picks tiles knowing the true flood map. An upper bound, not a usable policy.
Assam replays: the agent tasks tiles over real 2016 and 2022 events. Reference maps are model-derived and the forecast is an ERA5 pseudo-forecast.
